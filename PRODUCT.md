# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Independent Filmmakers & Emerging Talent**: Directors, screenwriters, actors, editors, cinematographers, and crew seeking recognition, workshops, and festival submission avenues.
- **Cinema Enthusiasts & Audiences**: Viewers discovering curated short films, exclusive interviews, podcasts, and indie cinema journalism.
- **Industry Professionals & Producers**: Talent scouts, distribution partners (e.g. Verus Productions), and experienced mentors looking to collaborate with emerging storytellers.

## Product Purpose
Viora is a premium cinema ecosystem that connects creators, audiences, and industry professionals. It democratizes independent film distribution, provides practical academy training, hosts short film festivals, and showcases original media under one unified platform.

## Positioning
Unlike single-purpose festival submission portals or standalone film blogs, Viora integrates the entire filmmaker journey—from learning (Academy), showcasing (Festivals & Laurels), and networking (Community), to distribution & production (Viora Originals & Acquisition Partners).

## Operating Context
- Web marketing & showcase platform accessible across mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
- Direct festival submission workflow for short films and screenplays (`/viorasfs`).
- Dual-mode application shell incorporating both public marketing screens and an internal administrative dashboard (`admin.vioramedia.in` / `?admin=true`).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Capabilities**: Festival submissions, digital laurels & accolades, academy course showcase, original media streaming previews, partner network.
- **Constraints**: Dark-mode aesthetic lock (deep obsidian `#000000` / `#0A0A0A` base with brushed gold `#C5A059` accents). Single-page React SPA with Vite and React Router v6.

## Brand Commitments
- **Tagline**: *"Where Every Story Finds Its Stage."* / *"Where Vision Becomes Art."*
- **Pillars**: Vizhi (Vision), Innovation, Oli (Light), Rhythm, Art.
- **Visual Identity**: Obsidian dark backdrop, brushed gold gradients, serif display typography (`Playfair Display`), and responsive physics-based micro-interactions (`motion/react` + `lucide-react`).

## Evidence on Hand
- **Acquisition Partner**: Verus Productions.
- **Current Surfaces**: HomeScreen, AboutScreen, FestivalsScreen, VioraSFSScreen, PartnerScreen, ContactScreen, AdminDashboard.

## Product Principles
1. **Unite the Ecosystem**: Bring learning, showcase, networking, and distribution into a single cohesive experience.
2. **Elevate Independent Creators**: Remove traditional gatekeepers and celebrate emerging storytellers with equal prominence.
3. **Cinema-Grade Quality**: Maintain a dark-tech, cinematic aesthetic with precise typography, contrast, and tactile motion engineering.
